    How to Play

    Tetris is a fast-paced tile-matching puzzle game where strategy meets speed. Your goal is to arrange falling geometric shapes (Tetrominoes) into solid horizontal lines. Once a line is completed, it clears from the board, granting points and creating more space for incoming pieces.

    Game Modes
    • Score Attack: Highest score wins when a player tops out. Every 10 lines increases the level and drop speed.
    • Time Attack: A 3-minute sprint. Maximize your efficiency and score as high as possible before time runs out.
